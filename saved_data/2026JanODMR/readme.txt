This folder contains ODMR data taken with a confocal microscope 
The experimental apparatus consists of two permanent magnets separated by a distance r. An NV-rich diamond chip/nanodiamond ensemble is located at roughly r/2.
The two magnets are mounted on a rotation arm that can rotate in the y-z plane.

Each filename contains information of the rotation angle (xx deg, with 00 deg being the arm in y-axis) and the distance between the magnet mount to the end of the rotation arm (yy mm).
The magnet separation r can be calculated as r = 2*(85-yy)

Folder "2026JanChip" contians ODMR taken on a E6 diamond chip.
Folder "2026JanChipHN" contians ODMR taken on a E6 diamond chip, but with higher noise.
Folder "2026JanND" contians ODMR taken with nanodiamond ensemble.
